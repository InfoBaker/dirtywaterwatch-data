# Data licence

The licence for the compiled dataset is being decided by the project. Until it is stated here, the compiled files
are published for review and non-commercial reuse with attribution to DirtyWaterWatch and to each source named in
the records, and every source's own terms apply to its records:

- Federal data (EPA ECHO and ICIS-NPDES, USGS NHD and NLDI, NOAA MRMS, NWS) is in the public domain
  (17 U.S.C. 105).
- State and local data carries the terms each agency states; those terms are quoted in `data/sources/<id>.yaml`
  (`license`). Where a source discourages redistribution, its records are left out of the release.
- The pipeline code is released under the MIT licence (see `LICENSE`).

The candidate licences for the compiled dataset are CC BY 4.0 and ODbL, with per-source attribution either way.
