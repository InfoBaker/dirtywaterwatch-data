# Draft: Maryland Public Information Act requests to MDE (three western Maryland CSO permits)

Channel: MDE's PIA portal, https://mdepia.mde.state.md.us/ ("Request Public Documents Under the Public Information
Act"; up to five requests at a time, site-specific by facility name and address). MDE's PIA page says "Please do not
submit requests via email" (staging copy of the page, read 2026-11-15); its FAQ lists the PIA coordinator's email for
questions only. Fees: the first two hours of search are free; MDE gives notice before fees pass $25 (MDE PIA FAQ,
https://mde.maryland.gov/programs/Marylander/PublicInfoAct/Pages/piafaq.aspx, read 2026-11-15).

File three site-specific requests, one per facility. Fill in the requester fields yourself in the portal.

---

## Request 1: Cumberland

- Request type: site-specific
- Facility name: Cumberland Wastewater Treatment Plant (John J. DiFonzo Water Reclamation Facility), Mayor and City of
  Cumberland
- Site address: 400 East Offutt Street, Cumberland, MD 21502 (Allegany County)
- Permit numbers: NPDES MD0021598; State Discharge Permit 06DP0567

Description of records:

> Under the Maryland Public Information Act, I request electronic copies of the following records for the Cumberland
> Wastewater Treatment Plant / John J. DiFonzo Water Reclamation Facility (NPDES MD0021598, State Discharge Permit
> 06DP0567):
>
> 1. The current discharge permit (including any permit administratively continued after its 2020 expiration date)
>    and its fact sheet, specifically any part that lists the combined sewer overflow (CSO) outfalls 002, 003, 005,
>    007, 008, 010, 011, 012, 015, 017 and 019.
> 2. Any table, list or map, in the permit, fact sheet, permit application, Long Term Control Plan or a later
>    submission to MDE, that gives each CSO outfall's number, location (latitude and longitude, or the coordinates as
>    recorded) and receiving water.
>
> If the outfall locations are in one document, that document alone satisfies this request. Electronic copies (PDF)
> are preferred. I am willing to pay fees up to $25; please tell me before incurring more.
