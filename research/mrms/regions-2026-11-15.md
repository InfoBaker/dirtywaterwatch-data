# MRMS regional grids: what the service publishes (probed 2026-11-15, 13:15 to 13:30Z)

Service: `obs/mrms_qpe` ImageServer. Answers saved beside this file (`catalog-all.json`, `info-*-24H.json`,
`identify-1.json`, `stats-1.json`, `stats-2.json`, `export-1.json`); the exported rasters are test fixtures
(`pipeline/tests/fixtures/mrms-{alaska,carib,alaska-prudhoe}-qpe-24h-20261115T13Z.tif`).

- **Items**: `conus_`, `alaska_`, `hawaii_`, `carib_QPE_{01,03,06,12,24,48,72}H`, one catalog item each per hour.
  Footprints (outSR 4326): conus 130–60 W, 20–55 N; alaska 176–126 W, 50–72 N; hawaii 164–151 W, 15–26 N; carib
  90–60 W, 10–25 N (carib overlaps conus south of 25 N; identify at Key West answers both grids' items).
- **Storage**: every raster is Web Mercator (EPSG:3857), not 0.01 degree lat/lon: pixels 1,189 m (conus), 1,408 m
  (alaska), 573 m (hawaii), 1,125 m (carib). Service limits maxImageWidth 15,000, maxImageHeight 4,100.
- **Lattice**: an export on the pipeline's 0.01 degree EPSG:4326 lattice, locked to the region's item, equals
  identify locked to the same item at every pixel compared (5 wettest + every outfall hundredth), to the tenth, in
  Alaska (Juneau) and the Caribbean (San Juan). Georeference checks clean.
- **Timing**: all four regions run in the same hourly cycle; each hour's items are filed within about a minute of the
  conus item (e.g. 24H valid 12Z filed 12:08:19 conus, :21 alaska, :23 hawaii, :24 carib; ingested 12:21 to 12:23).
- **No data**: MRMS writes -3 where no radar sees (every pixel around Prudhoe Bay, 70.26 N, in alaska_QPE_*, all
  hours); the export's own noData (-9999) only outside the raster. Juneau and San Juan answered real values (0.0, dry).
- **Unit**: labelled inches, values are millimetres in each region: alaska_QPE_24H (13Z) max 93.3 over the region at
  0.02 deg; carib_QPE_24H (12Z) max 53.9, carib_QPE_72H 292 (impossible as inches); hawaii max 20.4 / 32.5 (no verdict).
  Values come in tenths. Gauges: PAJN (58.35472, -134.57611), TJSJ (18.45, -66.00), PHNL (21.3275, -157.94306) on
  api.weather.gov; PAJN reports precipitation groups (0.8 mm/h on 11-14), dry at the probe.
- **Registry recount (data main 6df4fa1)**: Alaska 3 open CSO rows (Juneau, AK0023213 002/003/004, 3 points); the
  Prudhoe Bay row (AKG320002 08AA) is a crude-oil pipeline in `excluded_not_sewage`, not loaded. Puerto Rico 12 rows
  (PR0021555, 8 points), all PRASA's own coordinates; the EPA row at 18.44075, -66.44075 is superseded by
  `data/outfalls-pr.json`; no row lacks coordinates. Hawaii, VI, GU, AS, MP: no open CSO rows.
